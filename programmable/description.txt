ATOMYX is building a Proof-of-Recycling network for the United States and Europe: drop-off points with IoT scales, AI sorting stations and partner recyclers, with each verified batch of waste recorded on-chain. ATMX is its token. Fair launch on Programmable launch protection v1: 100% of the 1,000,000,000 supply is placed in the Uniswap v4 pool at launch and locked forever; no team, presale, investor, airdrop or community allocation, and no vesting; the launch wallet makes one small opening buy. Launch protection: opening valuation at least USD 15,000 on the Chainlink ETH/USD feed; for the first 300 seconds every wallet, including the developer, can buy or receive at most 2% of the supply; the total buy fee starts at 99% and its extra part falls linearly to zero over 30 seconds, paid to the Programmable treasury (the launch wallet is exempt from that extra part only); sells pay normal fees. Fees on every swap, in ETH, rounded up per trade: 0.30% Programmable platform fee plus a fixed 2.5% creator fee on buys and on sells, both immutable, so 2.8% all-in after the first 30 seconds. Creator fees go to the Reclaim Vault, which anyone can trigger to collect and distribute in fixed shares: 80% to the project treasury wallet, which the team uses freely for development, operations and marketing, and 20% kept in the vault as the Recycling Rewards pool. The Recycling Rewards pool can only be paid out, in ETH, against a waste batch recorded in the vault's on-chain Recycling Ledger: the attester records each batch with its material, its weight in grams and the hash of its three-checkpoint weighing evidence; a batch can be paid only after a 24-hour public review period, only if the guardian has not voided it, and in total at most its weight times the reward cap, which is {MAX_ETH_PER_KG} ETH per kg at launch and can never exceed 0.001 ETH per kg. The drop-off points, sorting stations and recycling partners are planned and not yet operating; nothing in the launch graph depends on them.
